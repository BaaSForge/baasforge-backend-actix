# BaaSForge Code of Conduct

## Purpose and scope

BaaSForge welcomes users and contributors with different backgrounds and levels
of experience. Help make it a place where people can ask questions, learn, and
work together without harassment or discrimination.

This policy applies across the BaaSForge backend, runtime, and frontend
repositories, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and other official
project spaces. It also applies when someone represents BaaSForge publicly.
Sponsorship, employment, contribution history, or maintainer status does not
exempt anyone from these expectations.

## Working together

- Review code and ideas respectfully. Explain the problem, its impact, and a
  possible improvement; avoid judging the person who wrote it.
- Give newcomers room to learn. Link the relevant setup or contribution guide
  and ask for missing information without ridicule.
- Keep disagreements focused on the project. Accept that maintainers may ask
  for a smaller change, compatibility work, or more evidence before merging.
- Respect people's time and boundaries. Do not repeatedly demand reviews,
  contact someone after being asked to stop, or pressure volunteers for support.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and correct misleading claims about checks,
  security findings, or project behavior.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without
permission are unacceptable. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern
or participates in a review is also unacceptable.

## Project-specific expectations

Use the contribution guide for the repository you are changing. Keep technical
feedback reproducible and distinguish observed behavior from assumptions.
AI-assisted contributions are subject to the same review and conduct standards;
the person submitting them remains responsible for their content.

Do not post production secrets, private project data, customer conversations,
or other people's credentials in issues, examples, screenshots, or logs.
Report vulnerabilities through the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md), rather than
publishing exploit details in a conduct report or public discussion.

## Reporting a concern

Email **[team@baasforge.com](mailto:team@baasforge.com)** privately. This is the
contact for conduct reports across all three repositories.

Include the relevant repository or project space, what happened, approximate
dates, and links or evidence you are comfortable sharing. Explain any immediate
safety or privacy concern and how you prefer to be contacted. Share only the
personal information needed to understand the report. Do not investigate other
people or contact them to gather evidence on the project's behalf.

If a report involves a maintainer or someone who may receive this mailbox,
state the conflict and request review by an uninvolved organization owner.
An involved person must not decide the outcome of their own case.

## Review and enforcement

BaaSForge organization owners and repository maintainers are responsible for
enforcement. They should limit access to reports to the people needed to review
and address them, and avoid disclosing a reporter's identity unnecessarily.
Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

Depending on the severity, impact, and repetition of the behavior, maintainers
may request a correction, issue a warning, remove content, restrict participation,
or impose a temporary or permanent ban. Serious incidents can warrant immediate
restrictions without a prior warning. Maintainers should explain decisions to
the affected participants when doing so does not compromise privacy or safety.

To request reconsideration, email the same contact with the decision and any
relevant new information. An uninvolved organization owner should review the
request. This policy does not set a guaranteed response deadline.
